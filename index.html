5 Grand Joseon Royal Palaces & UNESCO World Heritage in Seoul

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁): The primary royal palace of the Joseon Dynasty, featuring Geunjeongjeon Hall, Gyeonghoeru Pavilion, and the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony. Free admission when wearing traditional Hanbok.
  • Changdeokgung Palace & Huwon Secret Garden (창덕궁과 후원): UNESCO World Heritage site renowned for its harmony with nature.
  • Deoksugung Palace (덕수궁): Famous for its stone-wall road (Doldam-gil) and fusion of traditional Korean and Western neoclassical architecture.
  • Changgyeonggung Palace (창경궁) & Gyeonghuigung Palace (경희궁): Historic royal residences offering serene night tours and seasonal foliage.

Nationwide Seasonal Festivals in South Korea (전국 4계절 대표 축제)

  • Spring: Jinhae Gunhangje Cherry Blossom Festival (진해군항제), Yeouido Spring Flower Festival, Boseong Green Tea Festival.
  • Summer: Boryeong Mud Festival (보령머드축제), Busan Sea Festival, Daegu Chimac Festival.
  • Autumn: Andong International Mask Dance Festival (안동국제탈춤페스티벌), Jinju Namgang Yudeung Lantern Festival (진주남강유등축제), Jeonju Bibimbap Festival.
  • Winter: Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival (화천산천어축제), Taebaeksan Snow Festival, Haeundae Light Festival.
